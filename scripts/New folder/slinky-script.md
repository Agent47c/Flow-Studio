## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (Ready for ElevenLabs / Audio Recording)

```text
Did you know that the Slinky was invented by a naval engineer trying to stabilize sensitive battleship instruments?

In 1943, naval engineer Richard James was working at a Pennsylvania shipyard, designing heavy-duty tension springs to protect delicate instruments on rocking warships.

While reaching for a tool, he accidentally bumped a loose steel spring off a high shelf. But instead of crashing to the floor...

...it gracefully somersaulted end-over-end, walked down a stack of textbooks, stepped onto the floor, and coiled itself right back up. He stood there frozen in shock.

He took it home to his wife Betty. She combed through the dictionary for a word meaning sleek and graceful, and discovered 'Slinky.' Together, they took out a five-hundred-dollar loan to manufacture it.

In Christmas of 1945, they set up a sloped wooden ramp at Gimbels department store in Philadelphia. Richard gave one tiny nudge—and the crowd went completely wild.

Every single one of their four hundred Slinkys sold out in ninety minutes flat. Parents were fighting each other in the aisles to grab a box.

Today, over three hundred and fifty million Slinkys have marched down stairs across the planet—all because an engineer bumped a spring off his naval workbench.

So the next time you watch that coiled spring walk down your stairs... did you know that the Slinky was invented by a naval engineer trying to stabilize battleship instruments?
```
