## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs Ready)

**Voice Profile:** Male, warm, witty British/Mid-Atlantic storyteller (135 WPM).

> "In 1908, drinking tea was a messy, frustrating chore. You had to measure loose leaves, boil a pot, and scrub soggy leaf sludge out of metal strainers.  
> 
> Tea merchants shipped samples in heavy, expensive tin canisters. One New York merchant decided to cut postage costs with a clever shortcut.  
> 
> He had miniature pouches sewn from delicate golden silk, filled them with loose tea, tied them with string, and mailed them out as sample packs.  
> 
> His customers were supposed to cut open the silk bag, empty the leaves into a teapot, and discard the fabric. But customers didn't read instructions.  
> 
> Assuming it was a revolutionary new contraption, buyers simply grabbed the string and dunked the entire silk pouch right into boiling water.  
> 
> And it worked brilliantly! The porous silk let water infuse the leaves perfectly while trapping every single flake inside.  
> 
> No metal strainers, no soggy leaves stuck in your teeth, and zero cleanup: just lift the string, toss it, and drink.  
> 
> Orders flooded his shop. When he switched to porous gauze and later paper filter fiber, he didn't just save postage—he invented the modern tea bag.  
> 
> So the next time you steep a tea bag, remember: you're enjoying an invention that only exists because customers were too lazy to open a sample pouch..."
