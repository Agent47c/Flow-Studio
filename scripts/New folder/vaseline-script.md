## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs Ready)

**Voice Profile:** Male, deep, resonant, dramatic documentary pacing (135 WPM).

> "In 1859, Pennsylvania oil drillers cursed a foul black gunk called 'rod wax' that constantly jammed up their drilling machinery.  
> 
> Visiting chemist Robert Chesebrough noticed something bizarre: when drillers got nasty burns or deep cuts, they smeared the black sludge right over their wounds.  
> 
> And amazingly, their cuts healed rapidly without infection. Chesebrough filled jars with the foul grease and rushed back to his Brooklyn laboratory.  
> 
> For months, he distilled the extract, filtering it through charred bone charcoal to strip out impurities, dark color, and rancid odor.  
> 
> What emerged was a pure, odorless, translucent balm that locked in moisture and shielded skin from airborne bacteria: Petroleum Jelly.  
> 
> To prove it worked, he conducted extreme experiments on his own body: burning his skin with acid and fire, then applying the jelly to prove how fast it healed.  
> 
> The burns healed cleanly without a single scar. He patented the process and named it Vaseline, from the German word for water and Greek for oil.  
> 
> He toured New York in a horse cart giving out free jars. Within years, Vaseline was in every hospital, military kit, and household medicine cabinet.  
> 
> So the next time you use Vaseline on dry lips, remember: you're soothing your skin with what oil drillers used to scrape off filthy machinery..."
