# Complete Production Package: The Failed Carriage Tire That Became Chewing Gum

## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs Ready)

> "In 1869, an exiled Mexican general brought a chunk of dried jungle tree sap called *chicle* to a New York inventor, hoping to create synthetic rubber tires.  
>

>

>

> 
> Adams Chewing Gum exploded worldwide, inventing vending machines to dispense it and creating Chiclets—turning failed carriage tires into a multi-billion-dollar craze.  
> 
> So the next time you pop a piece of gum, remember: you're enjoying a failed rubber tire that was almost dumped into the river..."
