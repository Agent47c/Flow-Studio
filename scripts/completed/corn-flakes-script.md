## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (Ready for ElevenLabs / Audio Recording)

```text
Did you know that Corn Flakes were invented because two brothers accidentally left a pot of boiled grain to spoil for days?

In 1894, Dr. John Kellogg and his brother Will ran a strict health sanitarium in Michigan, trying to create bland digestible food for sick patients.

They boiled a batch of wheat to make bread, but were called away on emergency surgeries. The pot sat forgotten on the counter for days, turning stale and fermented.

Operating on a strict budget, Will refused to throw the ruined dough away. He shoved the stale lump through a set of metal rollers, hoping it would flatten into a sheet.

Instead of a smooth sheet, the stale grain shattered into hundreds of individual, paper-thin flakes. They toasted them in the oven—and they turned miraculously crisp.

They served the toasted flakes with cold milk to hospital patients. The patients went completely crazy for them, demanding them by the crate even after going home.

Will repeated the process with sweet corn—creating Corn Flakes. He added a pinch of sugar, bought out his puritanical doctor brother, and launched the Kellogg Company.

Today, the world eats billions of bowls of cereal every single year—all born from a pot of spoiled wheat that nobody wanted to throw in the trash.

So the next time you pour a bowl of crunchy flakes in the morning... did you know that Corn Flakes were invented from spoiled dough?
```
