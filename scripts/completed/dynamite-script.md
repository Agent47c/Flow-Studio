> "In 1860, nitroglycerin was the most powerful explosive on earth—and so unstable that a dropped bottle could level an entire city block.  
>

>

>

>

>

> 
> He wrapped the paste into cardboard cartridges and named it after the Greek word for power: *Dynamis*. Dynamite was born.  
> 
> Almost overnight, dynamite carved the Panama Canal, blasted tunnels through the Alps, and laid thousands of miles of continental railway.  
> 
> The fortune built on taming liquid death eventually funded the Nobel Peace Prize. And it all started because a deadly liquid..."
