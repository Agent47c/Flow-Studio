# Complete Production Package: The Toxic Coal Tar That Tasted Like Sugar (Saccharin)

**Title:** The Accidental Discovery of Saccharin  
**Format:** Vertical Short-Form Video (9:16)  
**Target Duration:** 65–75 Seconds

> "In 1879, a chemist working with toxic industrial coal tar derivatives was trying to create new synthetic dyes.  
>

> 
> In 19th-century chemistry, tasting chemicals was practically a death sentence. But his empty stomach made him careless.

> 
> He named it Saccharin. When World War sugar rations hit decades later, it kept nations sweet—and launched the multi-billion-dollar diet industry.  
> 
> So the next time you drop a pink packet into your morning coffee, remember: it only exists because a tired chemist forgot to wash off toxic industrial sludge..."
