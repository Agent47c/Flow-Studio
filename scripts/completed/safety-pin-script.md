## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs Ready)

**Voice Profile:** Male, punchy, ironic, engaging storyteller pace (138 WPM).

> "For centuries, fastening clothes meant using straight pins: dangerous iron needles that tore dresses, stabbed fingers, and constantly poked babies.  
> 
> In 1849, prolific inventor Walter Hunt owed a friend fifteen dollars—about five hundred dollars today—and had zero money to pay it.  
> 
> Desperate for quick cash, he sat at his workbench, picked up an eight-inch piece of scrap brass wire, and began fidgeting with it while brainstorming.  
> 
> He twisted the center of the wire into a coiled spring loop, giving the two arms natural tension so they would snap back when pressed.  
> 
> Then came the genius stroke: he curled the tip into a protective clasp cover that buried the needle point, making it completely impossible to prick your skin.  
> 
> In less than three hours of fidgeting, he had invented the Safety Pin. It was safe, flexible, and completely foolproof.  
> 
> He patented it immediately, but wanting to clear his debt fast, he sold the entire patent rights outright for four hundred dollars. He paid off his friend and kept three hundred and eighty-five bucks.  
> 
> The company that bought the patent manufactured billions of safety pins, earning millions upon millions while Hunt never saw another dime.  
> 
> So the next time you use a safety pin, remember: one of the most profitable designs in history was created in three hours just to pay off a fifteen-dollar debt..."
