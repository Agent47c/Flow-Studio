## 🎙️ Complete Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs Ready)

**Voice Profile:** Female or Male, lively, modern narrative pacing, engaging and upbeat (138 WPM).

> "In 1953, scientists at 3M were trying to invent a new heat-resistant rubber hose for supersonic jet engines.  
> 
> During an experiment, an assistant dropped a glass beaker, splashing a brand new synthetic fluorochemical compound all over the floor.  
> 
> A huge glob landed right on the canvas toe of researcher Patsy Sherman's clean white tennis shoes.  
> 
> She scrubbed it with soap, rubbed it with alcohol, and soaked it in harsh industrial solvents. Nothing could budge the polymer.  
> 
> She kept wearing the shoes anyway. Weeks later, she noticed something crazy: while the rest of the canvas got filthy with lab dust and grease, the spilled spot stayed blindingly snow-white.  
> 
> She tested it with coffee, grease, and motor oil. The liquids couldn't soak into the fabric threads—they formed frictionless beads and rolled right off like marbles.  
> 
> The jet fuel rubber had failed, but they had accidentally invented the world's most powerful stain repellent: Scotchgard.  
> 
> It revolutionized furniture, carpets, and jackets, saving millions of living rooms from disastrous spills for generations.  
> 
> So the next time your spilled coffee wipes right off your couch without leaving a mark, thank the ruined tennis shoe that was never supposed to exist..."
